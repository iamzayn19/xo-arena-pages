Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains what XO Arena: Grid Rivals ("XO Arena", "we", "us") collects when you play, why, and what choices you have. XO Arena is built on Firebase (Google) for authentication, game state, and diagnostics, and Google AdMob for ads.

Information we collect

  • Account identity. Every player gets an anonymous Firebase account automatically — no sign-up is required to play. If you choose to save your progress, you may additionally link Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, an email address, or a passkey. We store whatever identifier that method provides (e.g. your email address, or an opaque Apple/Google account identifier) — never your password, which Apple/Google never share with us at all.
  • Profile & gameplay data. Your display name, an optional profile photo you upload, trophy count, coins/gems balance, match history, and cosmetic items you own — all stored so your progress persists across devices.
  • Match & chat content. Moves you make and messages you send in an active match's chat, stored only for the lifetime of that match plus a short retention window, and visible only to the opponent you're playing.
  • Reports and blocks. If you report or block another player, we store who reported/blocked whom and why, to enforce fair play. Reports are reviewed manually.
  • Gameplay analytics. Anonymous event data (e.g. a match started, a daily reward was claimed) used to understand how the game is used in aggregate. We never send your email, display name, join codes, or any authentication token in an analytics event.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics collects a crash report (device model, OS version, stack trace) to help us fix it. Log messages from sensitive areas of the app (sign-in, matchmaking, payments, and similar) are redacted before any crash report is generated.
  • Device integrity signals. To prevent cheating and abuse, your device provides an attestation token (Apple App Attest, via Firebase App Check) and a cryptographic signature from a key that never leaves your device. Neither identifies you personally — they only prove a request came from a genuine, unmodified copy of the app.
  • Advertising data. XO Arena shows ads via Google AdMob (rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional interstitials between sessions — never during a match). We do not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), so ads are served without your device's advertising identifier and are not personalized based on your activity in other apps. Google may still use non-personalized signals (like your device's general region and Apple's SKAdNetwork) to serve and measure ads — see Google's own policy, linked below.

How we use this information

  • To operate the game: matchmaking, saving progress, and enforcing rewards server-side so nothing can be faked by a modified client.
  • To keep the game fair: detecting cheating, resolving reports, and enforcing blocks.
  • To fix bugs and improve the game, using aggregate analytics and crash data.
  • To show ads and, where you explicitly choose to watch one, credit the reward it grants.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for anything beyond what's described here.

Who we share it with

Your data is processed by Google/Firebase (our hosting and backend infrastructure) and Google AdMob (for ads). If you link Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, those providers naturally see that you're signing into XO Arena. We do not otherwise share your data with third parties, and we never sell it.

Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time from Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, wallet, stats, and inventory.
  • Block or report another player directly from a match.
  • Remove your profile photo at any time from Edit Profile.

Children's privacy

XO Arena is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Match chat is deleted shortly after a match ends. Deleting your account removes your data outlined above; some records (e.g. reports filed against you, for fair-play integrity) may be retained in de-identified form.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of XO Arena after a change means you accept the updated policy.
